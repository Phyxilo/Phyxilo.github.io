01 — About

I'm a computational physicist: I care as much about the analysis code as about the physics it reveals.

I studied physics at Middle East Technical University (BSc, MSc) and am now doing my PhD there. Since 2022 I've been part of the DsTau collaboration at CERN, where I've worked on everything from chemically developing nuclear emulsion films and running beam irradiations to scanning at Nagoya University and analysing the experiment's first results.

Software has been part of the story from the start. I learned C, C++ and C# through game development — building games with a team since 2018 and writing a small physics engine in OpenGL — and today I bring that to ROOT, Geant4 and machine-learning pipelines, and to LLM systems for companies.
